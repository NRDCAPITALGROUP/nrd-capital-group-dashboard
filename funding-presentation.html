02 How it works

How does 0% interest funding actually work?

It sounds too good to be true, so here's exactly what's happening.

STEP 01

Banks compete for you

Banks compete hard for good business customers, and 0% introductory APR is one of their favorite tools.

STEP 02

We stack the promos

We sequence applications across the right banks, in the right order, so multiple 0% offers add up to real capital.

STEP 03

Your capital goes to work

Put the money into something that earns a return before the promo ends, so it works for you instead of costing you.

06

Keeps your monthly cash flow free.

Balance transfers

Move balances to extend your 0% runway.

Reporting and terms vary by issuer. Most business cards require a personal guarantee and a personal credit check at application.
