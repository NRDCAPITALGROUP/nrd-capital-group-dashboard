How does 0% interest funding work?

It sounds too good to be true, so here's exactly what's happening.

Banks compete hard for good business customers. One of the main ways they compete is with aggressive promotional terms, like 0% introductory APR on business credit cards and lines of credit.

By sequencing applications across the right banks, in the right order, with your profile positioned correctly, we work to stack those promotional offers into a meaningful pool of capital, often for 12+ months at 0%.

After the promo period the rate goes up. The play is to put that capital to work in something that produces a return before it does, so the money works for you instead of costing you.

Phase 1
No-doc funding

Your first round. The goal is to work toward up to in 0% business credit, typically without tax returns or bank statements.

Phase 2
Bank statement funding

Deploy round-one capital into a cash-flowing business or investment. Those deposits become the bank statements that unlock larger lines.

Phase 3
Full-doc funding

With statements and business tax returns, approach banks and credit unions for larger term loans and lines tied to your revenue.
